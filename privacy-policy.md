# DigiSpin privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-08

## Summary

DigiSpin keeps everything on your device. It does not collect, send, or share
any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no network
features.

## What DigiSpin stores on your device

DigiSpin saves the following locally, in the app's own storage on the device:

- Your progress: which levels you have solved, and the moves and hints used on each.
- Your statistics and achievements.
- Your settings: the light, dark or device theme, the digit colour theme, and
  whether the move counter is shown.

This data never leaves the device. It is removed when you delete the app, or
when you use reset progress in Settings.

## What DigiSpin does not do

- It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.
- It does not connect to any server to send or receive data during play.
- It does not track you across other apps or websites.
- It does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

## Children

DigiSpin does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting or sending data, this policy will be updated
before that change ships, and the store listings will be updated to match.
